Routed experts' row kernel (core/row_projection tf_row_projection_indexed), decode row, 6 picks a layer.
M5 Ultra 64-core GPU, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 TF-iQ-heretic, engine f3d8e5e4 + patches 0002-0003.

== the kernel alone, one matrix of each of the 40 layers in turn (1.06 GB a pass, past the caches; DS_SWEEP=kbench)
w1 (2304 x 5120, 2/3-bit)  with a barrier before each:  33.0 us a layer,  805 GB/s
w1                          overlapped (no barriers):   24.5 us a layer, 1082 GB/s
w2 (5120 x 2304, 2/3-bit)  with a barrier before each:  34.2 us a layer,  776 GB/s
w2                          overlapped:                 26.1 us a layer, 1018 GB/s
DRAM peak 1228 GB/s: overlapped, the kernel runs at 83-88% of it. A barrier before each dispatch costs ~8.5 us.

== conclusion
The routed-expert kernel is not the bottleneck: on its own it reads at 83-88% of peak bandwidth. Its 5.3 ms in the
leave-out sweep includes the barriers before its 120 dispatches a token. The time a token loses is the ~8.5 us
each of its 764 dependent dispatches waits for the one before to drain.
