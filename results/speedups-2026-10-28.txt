Decode speedups on the M5 Ultra (64-core GPU), DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 TF-iQ-heretic. Every step's decode-row logits
are byte-identical to the reference engine's (f3d8e5e4: 72a552ed + the residency patch), checked with tools/gate.sh:
a decode row's logits (DS_REF), 128 greedy tokens plain and DSpark-drafted against the reference's, and four shared
streams against each alone (108/108 rows, 32/32 drafts equal).
